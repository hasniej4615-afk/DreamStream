# DMStreaM v1.3 "Owl's Eye" Update

## What is Owl's Eye?
Owl's Eye is a major stability update for DMStreaM. It's a set of intelligent systems that run in the background while you watch movies, making sure you always get a working stream with zero ads.

## Key Features

### 🚀 Instant Playback
Owl's Eye monitors the loading process. If a video server is taking too long to respond or gets stuck on a black screen, the app will **automatically switch** to a faster mirror before you even notice.

### 🛡️ Smart Ad-Blocking (Nuker)
The new **Nuker Engine** is more aggressive than ever. It blocks hidden tracking scripts and pop-up ads at the network level, ensuring that the only thing you see is the movie.

### 🧟 Zombie Protection
Ever clicked a movie only to find it's just a 2-minute ad loop? Owl's Eye detects these "Zombie Streams" instantly and finds a real version of the movie for you automatically.

### 📺 TV Optimized
Browsing on Android TV is now much smoother. The app understands that TV processors are different from phones and gives the video player exactly the right amount of "breathing room" to load high-quality streams without stuttering.

### 🔍 Deep Sniffing
Our engine now scans the code of video websites to find direct high-definition links. This means **ExoPlayer** (the native player) can be used more often, resulting in better battery life and higher quality.
